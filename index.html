A letter I wanted you to keep

For you, always.

My love,

If you're reading this, I hope you know that you don't need a reason to be here. You don't need to be missing me particularly badly, having a difficult day, or searching for something to make you smile. You can come here simply because you want to, and that is reason enough.

I wish I could put into words exactly what you mean to me. Sometimes, even when I have a thousand things I want to tell you, words feel too small for everything I carry in my heart. But if there is one thing I hope you never have to question, it is how deeply you are loved.

I love you on the days when you're happy and everything feels right. I love you on the days when you're tired, when things don't go as planned, when you're quiet, when you overthink, and when you don't quite know how to explain what you're feeling. You never have to be a perfect version of yourself for me. You don't have to earn my affection by being cheerful, strong, or okay all the time.

You can just be you.

And I hope that, little by little, you learn to see some of the beautiful things in yourself that I see in you.

Sometimes, I wish distance could be a little kinder to us. I wish I could be there for the ordinary moments — to hear about your day without a screen between us, to sit beside you when neither of us has anything to say, to notice when you're tired, and to make the little things easier.

Until those days become part of our everyday life, I hope this little corner reminds you that you have a place in my heart that distance cannot turn into an unfamiliar place.

I made this archive because I don't want our moments to become things we vaguely remember someday. I want to keep the little details, the places, the conversations, the laughter, and the things that might seem insignificant to everyone else but mean something to us.

And somewhere among all those memories, I wanted there to be a place that tells you something simple:

You are loved even when nothing special is happening.

You don't have to wait for an anniversary, a beautiful photograph, a perfect day, or a grand gesture to be reminded of that. There is so much love in the ordinary days, too. In checking on you. In remembering the little things. In wanting to hear about your day. In making room for your feelings.

I know that neither of us can predict every detail of the future. But I know that what we build together matters to me. I want to keep learning you, understanding you, growing with you, and creating a life where we both feel safe enough to be ourselves.

I want us to have not only beautiful memories, but also the patience and tenderness to get through the difficult ones.

So, whenever you visit this page, please don't feel that you need to find anything new. Let it be enough that it exists. Let it remind you that somewhere, there is a person who is grateful that you are part of his life, who treasures the little pieces of your story that he gets to know, and who wants you to feel loved not only in the biggest moments, but in the quiet spaces between them.

And if I could leave you with just one thought, it would be this:

You don't have to do anything extraordinary to be precious to me. You don't have to prove anything. You don't have to wonder whether an ordinary version of you is enough.

You, exactly as you are today, are someone I am so grateful to love.

Whenever you need a little reminder, come back here.

There will always be more memories to keep, more little things to discover, and more life for us to experience together.

Until I can say all of this while sitting right beside you, let these words keep you company. With all my love, always. ∞

A little piece of my heart, kept here for you.
